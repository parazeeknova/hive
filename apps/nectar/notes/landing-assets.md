# Landing artwork

## hive-meadow.webp

Static hero replacement generated with the built-in image tool, with a quieter photographic direction.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: restrained wide hero photograph for Hive, a software product with a bee identity. A believable editorial photograph of a single honeybee resting on a small white chamomile flower, in the RIGHT third of a quiet meadow. The surrounding field is softly out of focus, with a few white daisies and delicate grass stems, a pale cream sky and muted olive greens. Soft overcast late-afternoon natural light, subtle warm honey tones, organic film grain, realistic tiny bee anatomy and scale, genuine botanical detail. Horizontal panoramic composition, generous quiet negative space on the left and upper middle, crisp detail limited to the bee and one flower on right. Calm, understated, premium nature photography. No fantasy landscape, no miniature world, no fuzzy toy bees, no 3D render, no honeycomb architecture, no orange sunset, no floating sparkles or magical glow, no oversaturated colors, no repeated bees, no text or watermark.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The former `meadow.png` and `bee-left.png` served as visual references. Final assets are compressed WebP files in `public/landing/`; the page also reuses Hive's existing bees and coin artwork. No generated text or UI is baked into the images.

## Photographic section refinements

Twelve distinct editorial-style nature images were generated with the built-in image tool to match the static hero. Final assets are `public/landing/workflow-*.webp` (four workflow panels), `public/landing/creator-*.webp` (four creator chapters), and `public/landing/pill-*.webp` (four inline images). The exact prompt and saved path for each image are recorded in `landing-photo-prompts.json`.
